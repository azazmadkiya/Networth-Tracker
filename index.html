🔒 100% On-Device Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026 | App: Net Worth Vault

Key Commitment: Net Worth Vault operates on a 100% offline, zero-tracking model. We do not collect, transmit, monetize, sell, or share your financial numbers or bank balances with remote cloud servers or third parties.

1. Information Handled by the App

All records you enter remain strictly inside your device's private SQLite application sandbox:

  • Financial Accounts: Bank accounts, cash holdings, stocks, mutual funds, gold, and liabilities.
  • Family Profiles: Allocations categorized under Self, Father, Mother, or Family Joint.
  • Ledger Entries: Double-entry bookkeeping transactions, income deposits, and expense records.
  • Vault Passcode: Stored and verified locally using one-way cryptographic hashing.

2. Storage & Security Architecture

Your financial numbers are saved in a local SQLite / Room database. There are no remote user databases, tracking APIs, or cloud sync servers.

3. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

  • No Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry trackers.
  • No Advertising: No ads, interstitial popups, or Advertising ID (AD_ID) collection.
  • Zero Data Sharing: Your financial data is never shared with third-party brokers.

4. Device Permissions

Storage / File Access: Requested strictly when you choose to export or import your personal JSON backup files via Android's Storage Access Framework.

5. Contact & Support

For questions or verification regarding this policy, contact developer Azaz Madkiya at azazmadkiya@gmail.com.

Account & Data Deletion

Right to Erasure & Data Deletion Instructions

Immediate Local Erasure: Net Worth Vault does not host user accounts on remote cloud servers. You have complete control over all data on your device.

How to Delete Your Data

Method 1: Instant In-App Reset (Recommended)

  1. Open Net Worth Vault on your Android phone.
  2. Tap on the Settings tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone: Data Erasure & Reset card.
  4. Tap "Delete All Financial Data & Reset App".
  5. Confirm the prompt. All assets, bank records, ledger history, and security credentials will be permanently wiped.

Method 2: Uninstall the Application

Uninstalling the application from your Android device immediately deletes the entire local SQLite database sandbox.
